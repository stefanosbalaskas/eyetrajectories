How to interpret the tiers

Tier 1 · Core

Trajectory objects, preprocessing, functional distances and essential dense FDA. Expected to have a strong compatibility and numerical contract, but evidence remains method-specific.

Tier 2 · Advanced

Sparse data, regression, hierarchical analysis and uncertainty estimation. Data-generating conditions, dependence, estimands and assumptions must be reported explicitly.

Tier 3 · Experimental / specialized

Nonlinear methods, specialized workflows and optional bridges. Scientific claims must remain bounded to tested domains; source wrappers are not necessarily new estimators.
