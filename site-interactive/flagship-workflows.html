01 · CORE · DENSE FUNCTIONAL DATA

Complete gaze-trajectory analysis

Research question: How do aligned two-channel trajectories vary across experimental viewing conditions?

Generate 24 simulated participants viewing two conditions, construct two-channel trajectories on an explicit common grid, verify L² matrix symmetry, fit MFPCA and inspect scores and low-rank reconstruction.

Methods: etr_trajectory_set, etr_pairwise_l2, etr_fit_mfpca, etr_fpca_scores, etr_reconstruct_fpca.

Boundary: normalized time is not original sample time, synthetic x/y are not calibrated gaze angles, and descriptive FPCA does not test a condition effect.

02 · ADVANCED · REPEATED-MEASURES STRUCTURE

Descriptive multilevel functional variation

Research question: How much variation is expressed across participant mean curves versus repeated trial deviations?

Generate 30 simulated people each with four repeated functional observations, fit the native descriptive multilevel FPCA model and visualize between-subject and within-subject functional strata.

Methods: etr_fit_multilevel_fpca, etr_multilevel_scores, etr_multilevel_variance.

Boundary: this decomposition is not an inferential functional mixed-effects model. The two reported strata are not automatically additive population variance components.

03 · ADVANCED · IRREGULAR OBSERVATIONS

Sparse trajectory recovery under known truth

Research question: How accurately can a sparse functional estimator recover a trajectory when time support is irregular?

Create 80 synthetic irregular curves with 7–12 observations each, estimate a sparse PACE model with the optional fdapace R backend and compare reconstruction with its independent analytic generating process and a mean-only baseline.

Methods: etr_sparse_curves, etr_fit_sparse_pace, etr_sparse_pace_fitted, etr_sparse_pace_scores.

Boundary: PACE estimation is delegated to fdapace; one-factor known-truth recovery is not a proof of performance for arbitrary missingness or sensor dropout.

04 · ADVANCED · PARTICIPANT-LEVEL INFERENCE

Uncertainty-aware paired comparison

Research question: What is the estimated population mean participant-level functional contrast between two repeated experimental conditions?

Generate 32 participants completing three trials per condition, compute each participant's paired B−A functional contrast, then construct participant-resampled pointwise and simultaneous mean bands.

Methods: etr_trajectory_set, etr_bootstrap_mean_band.

Boundary: the single example does not establish 95% coverage. Separate simulation experiments found missingness-sensitive empirical coverage, so participant dependence and missingness must be reported.

Reproducibility contract for all four examples

Each complete run creates three PDF figures, design/estimate CSV files, the generated methods and limitations report, and the R session manifest. Source files are versioned in the repository, and website figures are generated from those R scripts during the site build. The input datasets are generated deterministically by the examples and are not copied from OneStop or PoTeC.

To run the scripts yourself, install the exact 0.4.3 GitHub release and the optional R package fdapace for workflow 03. All generated outputs should be considered illustrative until checked against an external research design and its data-generating assumptions.
