01 / structural data assessment

Inspect a local CSV without uploading it.

Check actual recorded support before selecting dense, sparse, asynchronous or repeated-trial methods. Parsing occurs entirely in your browser. The tool neither transmits nor stores participant-level rows.

Long-format gaze data

Browser-local: no fetch, tracking, upload or server-side analysis. Maximum 12,000 data rows and 1.2 MB of CSV text. Summary export excludes participant IDs, trial IDs, raw values and file names. File content is held only in this browser tab until cleared or reloaded.

Choose a local CSV, paste text, or open the invented sample.

Structural preflight

Required columns: participant_id, trial_id, x, y, and one of time_s, time_ms or time. Additional columns remain unprocessed. Missing coordinates are not zero.

A shared set of time points is only a structural prerequisite for dense common-grid FPCA. Nonpaired or missing x/y values cannot establish true sensor-level asynchronous acquisition. The inspector cannot determine whether missing gaze is random or assess eye-tracker calibration, drift, AOI assignment, model fit or inferential calibration.
