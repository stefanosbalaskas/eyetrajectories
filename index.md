NEW · VERIFIED REAL-DATA PERFORMANCE BENCHMARK

## Pairwise trajectory distances, 104–215× faster in the tested configurations.

On real OneStop fixation trajectories, the R-only weighted L² distance
optimization reduced the 1,000-curve, 128-point median from 31.91 s to
0.305 s, with 21/21 declared numerical checks passing. Explore all six
configurations, the numerical safeguards, and the release-version
boundary. This is a development-branch benchmark, not a change to the
archived v0.4.3 software.

[Read the benchmark and limitations
→](https://stefanosbalaskas.github.io/eyetrajectories/SITE_PERFORMANCE.md)

BEYOND VISUALIZATION

## Three methods. Three ways to understand a trajectory.

A trajectory’s shape, timing, and return structure reveal different
information. Explore what changes when you adjust the data—and where
interpretation depends on modelling assumptions.

[φ₁(t) 01 · FUNCTIONAL DATA ANALYSIS **Functional PCA** Decompose
variation across whole curves and inspect reconstruction errors as more
components are retained. *Try the live example
→*](https://stefanosbalaskas.github.io/eyetrajectories/methods-laboratory.md)
[DTW 02 · TEMPORAL ALIGNMENT **Dynamic time warping** Change the phase
of two signals and trace the minimum-cost monotone alignment. *Try the
live example
→*](https://stefanosbalaskas.github.io/eyetrajectories/methods-laboratory.md)
[Rᵢⱼ 03 · NONLINEAR DYNAMICS **Recurrence analysis** Change state-space
radius and temporal exclusion, then inspect the recurrence matrix and
line statistics. *Try the live example
→*](https://stefanosbalaskas.github.io/eyetrajectories/methods-laboratory.md)

Educational browser implementations with synthetic trajectories. For
